## Welcome

Incremental games are built on a simple loop: earn something, spend it on upgrades, and earn more of it faster. Simple to describe, surprisingly hard to get right.

This site collects what I've learned about the genre while making my own games: what players expect, what makes these games feel good, and where to look for more. It's a work in progress, and it grows as I learn.

## Why "1e308"?

In JavaScript, and in most languages that store numbers as 64-bit floating point, a regular number can't go much beyond 1.8 × 10<sup>308</sup>, written as `1e308`. Incremental games reach that ceiling more often than you'd think, and getting past it is part of the fun. The [big numbers](/1e308/development/big-numbers/) page explains how.
