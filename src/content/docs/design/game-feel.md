In an incremental game, players collect resources thousands of times. Small details in how that feels add up quickly, for better or worse.

## Many small rewards beat one big one

Watching ten low-value coins fly across the screen is far more satisfying than seeing a single high-value coin.

So instead of giving the player one item worth 100 points, give them 100 items worth one point each. The total is the same, but the moment feels much bigger.

## Make collection sounds easy on the ears

The sound of collecting a single resource will play over and over, so it needs to be satisfying without ever becoming tiresome. Avoid anything too high-pitched.

A good reference is popcorn popping, at a slightly lower volume. When the player collects many resources at once, you can raise the pitch a little to add excitement.

:::caution
Put a cap on that pitch increase. Without one, a big burst of rewards quickly turns into a shrill noise.
:::
