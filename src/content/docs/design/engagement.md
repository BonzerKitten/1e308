Incremental games are repetitive by nature. That's not a flaw, since the loop is what players come for, but it helps to give them reasons to stay curious.

## Add a touch of luck

Luck-based mechanics create the same kind of excitement and curiosity that keeps people at a casino table. Random drops, surprise rewards or a chance at a bigger payout all give players something to look forward to.

Include something like this in at least one element of your game, but don't overdo it. The player's choices should still matter more than the dice.

## Use a little lore

A little lore goes a long way toward disguising how repetitive the game is. A few lines of story or a world worth wondering about gives players a reason behind the loop, and makes the hundredth run feel like part of something larger.
