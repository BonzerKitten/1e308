Watching someone else play is a quick way to discover new games and to see how players react to them in real time.

## Good places to start

- **[Idle cub](https://www.youtube.com/@idlecub)**: plays incremental and idle games, often new indie releases. [Upgrading my Battlemage to Destroy Hordes of Skeletons!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Os7He0zErJY) is a good place to start.
- **[Skoottie](https://www.youtube.com/@Skoottie)**: plays incremental games alongside other indie titles.
- **[Gohjoe](https://www.youtube.com/@Gohjoe)**: gaming videos focused mostly on indie games and roguelites.
- **[ImCade](https://www.youtube.com/@ImCade)**: plays small indie games, incremental games included.

:::note[About this list]
Every channel below was checked against its description and video titles to confirm it has played incremental or idle games. Subscriber counts are from September 2026 and will drift over time.
:::
