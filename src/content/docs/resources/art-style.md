Incremental games don't need expensive art to look great. A strong, consistent style often matters more than detail, and it keeps production manageable for a small team.

- **MS Paint scribble style:** Jerbob's devlog [i added the GOOFIEST NPCs to my Vampire Game](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o_Ivw8nBbx0) shows how charming a deliberately rough, hand-scribbled look can be.
- **Limited neon palette:** Idle cub's video on *Wild Growth*, [A Game Where All You Do Is Heal But It's Great!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wncc58mS-eM), shows a small set of neon colors carrying an entire game.
