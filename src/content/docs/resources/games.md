Playing is the best way to understand the genre. Several of these are short incremental games that take a few hours to finish, which makes them a great way to see a complete progression curve from start to end.

## Mining and digging

- **[Shelldiver](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3862670/Shelldiver/)**: a cozy game about a senior turtle starting a business selling jellyfish, upgrading gear and exploring underwater biomes.
- **[Diggin](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4157430/Diggin/)**: drill into a meteor crater, mine ores and uncover what happened to the moles that came before you.
- **[Space Rock Breaker](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4035270/Space_Rock_Breaker/)**: mine asteroids with guns, process ore by dropping it into a Plinko machine, and work through a massive skill tree.
- **[Astro Prospector](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3503440/Astro_Prospector/)**: an incremental bullet hell about mining coffee in space and upgrading your ship.

## Chopping and gathering

- **[Chop Chains](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4139720/Chop_Chains/)**: a relaxing game about chopping trees and upgrading axes and skills.
- **[It's Chopping Time!](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4272270/Its_Chopping_Time/)**: clear an island's forests using the forces of nature, from earthquakes to tidal waves.

## Combat and builds

- **[Maktala: Slime Lootfest](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4005560/Maktala_Slime_Lootfest/)**: fight hordes of slimes and use their loot to grow stronger.
- **[IncreKnight](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4305480/IncreKnight/)**: an incremental game about slaying a dragon.
- **[Increbraintal](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3863830/Increbraintal/)**: an incremental auto battler about protecting a brain from viruses, with a skill tree at its core.
- **[Outhold](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3767740/Outhold/)**: a short, strategic mix of tower defense and deep meta-progression.
- **[Rocket Rats](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3069810/Rocket_Rats/)**: a survivors-like where you fight cheese-based astral bodies in space, as a rat.
- **[Horripilant](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3525970/Horripilant/)**: an idle incremental dungeon crawl through a dark, forgotten underworld.

## Space and beyond

- **[Starvester](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4194800/Starvester/)**: expand a factory across a star system with swarms of drones and build giant megastructures.
- **[A Game About Feeding A Black Hole](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3694480/A_Game_About_Feeding_A_Black_Hole/)**: feed a black hole by destroying asteroids, planets and stars.

## Something different

- **[All Hail the Orb](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262310/_All_Hail_the_Orb/)**: a light-hearted pixel-art game about powering a mysterious orb, summoning cultists and slowly unlocking automation.
- **[Click and Conquer](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3267900/Click_and_Conquer/)**: a short, relaxing game about dropping bombs through the fog of war to uncover enemies and new maps.
