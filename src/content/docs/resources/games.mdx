Playing is the best way to understand the genre. Many of these are short games that take a few hours to finish, which makes them a great way to see a complete progression curve from start to end.

## About the bundle counts

Every game shows how many Steam bundles it's part of. A bundle is a set of games sold together at a discount, usually put together by the developers themselves.

If you're a developer looking for games to bundle with yours, the count is a good hint of how open each team is to the idea:

- <Badge text="30+ bundles" variant="success" size="small" /> clearly open to bundling
- <Badge text="10 to 29 bundles" variant="caution" size="small" /> open to it
- <Badge text="Under 10 bundles" variant="note" size="small" /> more selective, or just getting started

Counts were checked in September 2026 and change over time. The full list, sorted by bundle count, is at the [bottom of the page](#all-games-by-bundle-count).
